Pixel Art Normalizer

Drop in ripped or found pixel art. It undoes upscaling, removes half-transparency and snaps every pixel to the project palette (maps/references/sprite_palette.gpl). Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded, and nothing needs to be installed.
