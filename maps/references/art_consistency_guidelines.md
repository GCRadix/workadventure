\# How to keep the pixel art consistent



\## Perspective

Art is viewed from a top-down, slightly downward-angled perspective (so not perfectly top-down, where you'd see the top of people's heads).

Think of Pokémon for GB/GBA, Stardew Valley, most retro 2D Zelda games, etc.



\## Resolution \& Pixel Size

Tiles are standardized at 32x32.

There's no upscaling, so no single "art pixel" should be larger than 1x1.

\## Palette

\## Transparency

All-or-nothing, no half-transparency.
